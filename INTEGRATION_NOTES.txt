AutoTune AI Adaptive Sequence integration patch

Replace the matching files in your project with the files in this archive.
The patch:
- loads the tokenizer pinned to each candidate revision during execute mode;
- profiles the rendered chat-template sequence before make_plan/run_lora;
- copies fitting records unchanged;
- quarantines oversized or malformed records without truncation;
- writes an adaptive audit manifest and a derived dataset_manifest.json;
- preserves source_ids/source-disjoint split validation;
- routes train/validation/test to prepared split files;
- evaluates the held-out prepared test split only once after selection.

Preflight remains offline and does not download tokenizers; actual tokenizer
profiling happens during explicit --execute.

Semantic splitting is intentionally not automatic. Quarantined rows need
task-aware review; this patch does not synthesize or truncate answers.
